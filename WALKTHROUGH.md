# HFT Pyramid Terminal - Walkthrough

## How to Use the Terminal

### 1. Main Pyramid View
- **Live Visual Order Flow**: The pyramid displays real-time trade volumes partitioned into logarithmic size tiers.
- **Sell Aggression (Red, Left)** vs **Buy Aggression (Green, Right)**: Watch where institutional liquidity is hitting the order book.
- **Apex Tier**: The top layer reflects institutional whale prints with golden glowing visual indicators.
- **Interactive Layer Inspection**: Tap any layer of the pyramid to open the Tooltip Overlay, revealing exact trade count, volume split, and dollar value.
- **Whale Flow Stream**: Real-time tape at the top highlights high-conviction trades above the institutional threshold.

### 2. Quantitative Strategies Screen
- View live evaluation results from all 20 quant strategies.
- Filter by category: **Trend**, **Momentum**, **Order Flow**, **Volatility**, or **Arbitrage**.
- Toggle strategies on/off to customize consensus calculations.

### 3. Terminal Settings
- **Asset Switcher**: Type any crypto symbol (e.g., `ETHUSDT`, `SOLUSDT`) or choose from quick chips.
- **Multi-Exchange Aggregator**: Enable or disable Binance, Bybit, OKX, Kraken, and KuCoin feeds on the fly.
- **Engine Tuning**: Adjust the decay slider to speed up or slow down volume dissipation.
- **Database Maintenance**: Clear local SQLite trade history cache.
